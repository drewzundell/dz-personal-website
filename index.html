About

I’ve been behind a microphone since high school, and I’m working toward the play-by-play booth.

I grew up in Morgantown, West Virginia, announcing basketball games over the gym PA and cutting highlights for the Mountain East Conference. Now I study broadcast and digital journalism at Syracuse’s Newhouse School, write opinion columns for The Daily Orange, and race distance events for the Orange in cross country and track.

Running at the Division I level taught me to plan every hour of the day. Newsrooms taught me to hit a deadline. I bring both to every assignment.
